YouGlideTube is a fast, buttery-smooth YouTube client for Android. Videos start buffered before you press play, Shorts scroll without loading pauses, and the interface is dark-first with a blue-to-teal brand, a glass navigation pill and cards coloured from their own thumbnails.

Features:

- Play videos and songs from YouTube and YouTube Music
- Optional Google sign-in, with watch history that syncs with your YouTube account
- Feed previews: videos preview as you scroll, tap to continue from the preview
- Shorts with pre-buffering for pause-free scrolling
- Subscription notifications, in the app and as system notifications
- Background audio and Picture-in-Picture (PiP) video playback
- Built-in SponsorBlock to automatically skip sponsored segments
- Built-in DeArrow to replace clickbait titles and thumbnails
- Built-in Return YouTube Dislike (RYD)
- Download videos and audio for offline playback
- Dedicated music player with synchronized live lyrics
- Comments that work: post, reply, edit, delete and like
- Live chat: send messages and remove your own
- Android TV layout with a D-pad navigation rail
- Local recommendation engine that learns your preferences, on your device
- 100% on-device privacy with zero analytics or tracking
- Import subscriptions, playlists and history from YouTube, NewPipe and LibreTube
- Block specific channels and topics from appearing in your feed
- Adjust audio pitch and playback speed
- Local playlist and queue management
- Swipe gesture controls for volume and brightness
- Modern Material 3 interface
- Export and backup your data
